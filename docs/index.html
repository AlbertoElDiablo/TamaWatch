TamaWatch

A virtual pet for your Garmin watch. Walk, train and sleep well, and it grows with you. Free, for 105 watches.

1

Choose your region

Each region is its own TamaWatch, with its own creatures and its own game, so trying another never touches the one you have. Kanto is the original: installing it over an older TamaWatch keeps your game.

2

Find your watch

Not in the list? TamaWatch needs a colour Garmin watch with Connect IQ 3.2 or newer and enough memory for apps, so some models (black-and-white ones, and a few with very little app memory) can't run it.

3

Download its file

Download TamaWatch

Each watch model has its own file; the one for another model won't start. After it downloads, rename it to TamaWatch.prg (so a later update simply replaces it). To keep two regions on one watch, give the second its own name, such as TamaWatch-johto.prg.

4

Copy it onto the watch

  1. Connect the watch to the computer with its charging cable.
  2. Open File Explorer. The watch appears under This PC (as its name, or as a drive).
  3. Open Internal Storage (if shown), then GARMIN, then Apps.
  4. Copy TamaWatch.prg into Apps. If an older TamaWatch.prg is there, replace it: your pet and progress stay on the watch.
  5. Eject the watch and unplug it. It takes a moment to install.
  1. Install OpenMTP (free). macOS can't open Garmin watches on its own. Quit Garmin Express first if it's running.
  2. Connect the watch with its charging cable and open OpenMTP.
  3. On the watch's side, open GARMIN, then Apps.
  4. Drag TamaWatch.prg into Apps. If an older TamaWatch.prg is there, replace it: your pet and progress stay on the watch.
  5. Unplug the watch. It takes a moment to install.

Some older watches show up in Finder as a drive; then just copy the file into GARMIN/Apps there.

  1. Connect the watch to the computer with its charging cable.
  2. Open your file manager (Files, Dolphin, Thunar...). The watch appears in the sidebar under its name.
  3. Open Internal Storage (if shown), then GARMIN, then Apps.
  4. Copy TamaWatch.prg into Apps. If an older TamaWatch.prg is there, replace it: your pet and progress stay on the watch.
  5. Eject the watch in the file manager and unplug it. It takes a moment to install.

Older watches show up as an ordinary USB drive; newer ones use MTP. If the watch doesn't appear, install MTP support and plug it in again: sudo apt install gvfs-backends (Ubuntu, Debian, Mint), sudo dnf install gvfs-mtp (Fedora) or sudo pacman -S gvfs-mtp (Arch).

From a terminal instead: install jmtpfs, then mkdir -p ~/watch && jmtpfs ~/watch, copy the file into ~/watch/Internal Storage/GARMIN/Apps/ (or ~/watch/GARMIN/Apps/) and finish with fusermount -u ~/watch.

5

Open it

Pick your watch above to see where TamaWatch appears on it.

  1. The first time, choose your language, your trainer's name and look, and how many workouts you do in a usual week.
  2. Pick your first partner, and keep moving: steps, workouts and sleep all count.
  3. UP and DOWN move, START picks, BACK goes back, and holding UP (or the book on screen) opens the menu. The guide in the menu explains everything else.

How it plays

Latest updates
  • Pick your region. Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova, Kalos or Alola, one per app.
  • HP that carries over. A bar under your partner's name: battles take it, it refills in 15 minutes.
  • Care counts in battle. A hungry, dirty or unhappy partner misses more of its attacks.
  • Training games can find fruit, one piece at most, now and then.
  • No alerts mid-workout. Care reminders wait while you're recording an activity.
  • Workouts pay EXP by length and sport, so weights earn as much as cardio.
  • A result screen after every battle, won or lost: both sides, EXP, HP and more. A loss pays a tenth of a win's EXP.
  • No steps lost: steps from a day you didn't open the app (your long run!) now count for eggs, EXP and apples.
  • Fruit fills 40% more of the food bar.
  • Only your partner gets hungry. Team and Pokébox creatures recover to full within 24 hours (HP within 15 minutes) and keep their own care when they become partner.
  • Hunger at a watch's pace: a full food bar lasts about 12 waking hours, and an apple comes every 2,000 steps.
  • Battles pay like FireRed: each creature beaten is worth its species' EXP yield x its level / 7 (x1.5 from a trainer). Wild creatures come a few levels below, at or above your partner's, and a miss from poor care tells you why (hungry, wants a bath, wants to train).
  • A daily gift: 2 pieces of fruit the first time you open the app each day (missed days don't add up).
  • Asleep means asleep. Your partner doesn't get hungry while you sleep, and no alerts reach you then (nor in Do Not Disturb).
  • Each step counts once for eggs: a waiting egg hatches first, then steps count toward the next one.
  • Recorded activities count again. Watches that date them differently were skipping every workout; now each one pays.
  • Battle friends from any region. Trades stay within one region.
  • A new page at the start of the game points you to the in-app GUIDE. Worth a read!
HP

The bar under your partner's name is its HP. Battles take it, and it keeps what's left: your next battle (wild, gym or link) starts from there. A battle that costs 40% or more of its HP also costs some food, a quarter of what it lost.

HP refills by itself, from empty to full in 15 minutes. Each fruit gives some back at once (its favourite more), and so does every training game; a recorded activity gives back half the bar.

Good care wins fights

When food, hygiene or joy is under half, your partner's attacks miss more often: the lower they are, the more it misses, up to 4 attacks in 10 when all three are empty. Feed it, bathe it and keep training.

Fruit from training games

The three training games on the football button sometimes turn up a piece of fruit: an apple, a blue berry or an orange. One piece at most per game, and about one game in three.

Your activities

Every activity you record on the watch (5 minutes or more) pays EXP by its length and sport. Per minute: running or swimming 20, strength or gym 27, cycling 15, hiking 12, walking 8, anything else 15. Weights raise your heart rate less but are just as hard, so 45 minutes of weights pay like an hour's run or 80 minutes on the bike (about 1,200 EXP). It also fills your partner's joy, trains a stat (running speed, cycling and swimming defence, strength attack), gives a berry or an orange (one more for every 45 minutes) and gives back HP. Intensity minutes outside your recorded activities still give 10 EXP each. Care alerts wait while you're recording, so nothing buzzes mid-run.

Regions, battles and trades

Your watch holds one region. Link battles work with a friend playing any region: a creature your watch has no picture of shows as an orb in its type's colours, and fights with its real types and moves. Trades need the same region on both watches, because a creature can only live in its own region; the watch tells you so if yours differ. Both watches need version 1.1 or newer.

If something's not right

The watch shows an "IQ" icon with an exclamation mark

The file is for a different model. Check the exact model in your watch's settings (System > About), download that one and replace the file.

TamaWatch doesn't appear on the watch

Make sure the file is directly inside GARMIN/Apps (not in a folder of its own) and ends in .prg, then unplug the watch and give it a minute.

Online battles and trades don't connect

Online play goes through your phone: keep it near, with Garmin Connect running and internet on. "Nearby" play connects two watches directly but hasn't been tried on real watches yet.

Updating to a new version

Download the new file for your watch and replace the old TamaWatch.prg in GARMIN/Apps. Your pet, team and progress are kept. Before version 1.1 TamaWatch was Kanto only: choose Kanto to carry on with that game.

Switching region

Another region is a separate app with its own new game: your game in the first region stays on the watch, untouched, as long as you keep its file (or put it back later).

What does TamaWatch do with my data?

It reads your steps, activities, Body Battery and sleep on the watch to run the game, and sends nothing anywhere. The only exception is online battles and trades: their game messages (your pet's name, level and team) pass through the public service ntfy.sh while you play.
